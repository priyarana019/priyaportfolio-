Hello! I am Priya, a first-year student pursuing my Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science and Engineering. I was born and raised in the tranquil city of Rishikesh, Uttarakhand, India—the world-renowned gateway to the Garhwal Himalayas and the holy river Ganga.

Growing up amidst the natural majesty of the Himalayas taught me the value of quiet focus, persistent patience, and keen observation. These exact qualities drew me toward Computer Science: I love dissecting complex logic, understanding what happens under the hood when a program compiles, and writing clean, understandable code.

As a beginner engineer, I do not believe in taking shortcuts. Instead, I am immersing myself in procedural programming with C, scripting and automation with Python, and mastering the fundamentals of HTML5 and CSS3. I am enthusiastic about attending college workshops, participating in freshman hackathons, and learning something new every single day.
